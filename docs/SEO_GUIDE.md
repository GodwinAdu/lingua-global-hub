# SEO guide: getting First Grade Lingua Consult found on Google

The website is built to rank: every page is server-rendered with its own title, description, share image and structured data, and scores 100/100 for SEO in Google Lighthouse. What decides the final ranking is mostly **content, reviews and links**, which are listed below. Do sections 1–2 in the first week; 3–5 are ongoing.

## What the website already does

| Feature | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Unique title + description per page (editable: **Dashboard → Website → SEO**) | Control over how each page reads in Google results |
| Business data (LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService): name, address, phones, hours, map location, services, social links | Eligibility for the Google knowledge panel and local results. Built from **Site settings**, so keep them accurate |
| Article data on every post, Service data on every service, Book data on every ebook, breadcrumbs everywhere | Rich results (dates, breadcrumbs, images) in search |
| Branded share image for every page and post (or your cover photo) | Proper previews on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, X |
| `sitemap.xml` (with images), `robots.txt`, RSS feed at `/feed.xml` | Google finds new posts within hours |
| Twi posts marked as Twi (`lang="tw"`) | Twi articles shown to Twi searchers |
| Optimised, lazy-loaded images (WebP/AVIF), no layout shift | Speed, which is a ranking factor |

## 1. Google Search Console (30 minutes, do first)

1. Go to <https://search.google.com/search-console> and sign in with the business Google account.
2. **Add property → URL prefix** → enter the live address, e.g. `https://firstgradelingua.com`.
3. Choose **HTML tag**, copy the code (or the whole `<meta ...>` tag).
4. In the dashboard: **Administration → Site settings → Search engines → Google Search Console code**, paste, **Save**.
5. Back in Search Console click **Verify**.
6. **Sitemaps** → enter `sitemap.xml` → **Submit**.
7. For each important page (home, each service), paste its URL in the top bar → **Request indexing**.

Optional: do the same at <https://www.bing.com/webmasters> (it can import from Search Console) and paste its code in **Bing Webmaster code**.

## 2. Google Business Profile (most important for "near me" searches)

1. <https://business.google.com> → add the business: **First Grade Lingua Consult**.
2. Category: **Translator** (primary). Add: *Interpreter*, *Translation service*, *Audio visual consultant*, *Recording studio*.
3. Use **exactly the same** name, address and phone as in the website's Site settings (Google compares them).
4. Pin the office location in Danfa; add hours, website link, WhatsApp, services with descriptions, and 10+ real photos (office, team, studio, events).
5. Complete Google's verification (postcard, phone or video).
6. **Ask every happy client for a Google review** and reply to every review. Reviews are the biggest local ranking factor.

## 3. Content plan (ongoing)

Google ranks pages that answer what people search. Aim for **2–4 posts a month**:

- Service-focused articles: "How to get a certified Twi translation in Ghana", "What a conference interpreter costs in Accra", "Choosing a voice-over artist for a radio ad".
- Language and culture posts in **Twi and English** (fewer competitors, loyal readers).
- Case studies from the portfolio: client, language, problem, result.

For each post, in the editor:
- **Title:** the words people actually type, near the start.
- **Summary:** 1–2 sentences; it becomes the Google description.
- **Cover image:** a real photo (1200×630 works best for sharing).
- **Category and tags:** fill them in.
- **SEO title / description:** only if you want them to differ from the title/summary.

Keep each **service page** detailed (Website → Services): what's included, languages, typical turnaround, who it's for. Thin pages rank poorly.

## 4. Links from other websites

- List the business (same name/address/phone) on: Ghana Yellow Pages, BusinessGhana, Ghana Business Directory, ghanayello.com, Facebook Page, LinkedIn Company Page, Instagram.
- Ask partners, clients, NGOs and universities you've worked with to link to the website.
- Share every new post on WhatsApp status, Facebook and LinkedIn.
- Add the social links in **Site settings** so they appear in the footer and in the business data.

## 5. Check progress monthly

- **Search Console → Performance:** which searches show the site, clicks, average position.
- **Search Console → Pages:** fix anything listed as "not indexed".
- **Dashboard home → Readers:** most-read posts; write more like them.
- Expect results over **2–6 months**. Local searches ("translation services Accra") usually move first.
